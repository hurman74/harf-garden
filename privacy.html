Harf Garden privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Harf Garden is an Android app that helps children ages 3–6 hear, trace, count, and match letters and numbers in English and Urdu. The developer is Hurman. The package name is com.hurman.harfgarden.

The app collects no personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, and no accounts. It works offline. If a later version changes that, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact: hurman74@gmail.com

What stays on the phone

Stars, stickers, the chosen language, the volume, the music and sounds switches, and a note about an optional unlock are stored in a database on the device. Hurman does not receive that database. There is no account to sign in to, and there is no server copy of a child’s progress.

The public settings file

The whole garden is free. The app may read one public settings file over HTTPS:

https://hurman74.github.io/harf-garden/config.json

That request sends no identifiers, no cookies, no query parameters, and no custom headers. The file is a small JSON document. If the phone is offline, the request times out, the file is missing, or the file cannot be read, the garden stays free. The app does not wait on this request before it opens. It does not upload progress, a device id, or an advertising id.

Purchases

Optional Google Play purchases, if a grown-up is ever offered one, are handled by Google Play. Hurman does not receive card numbers, bank details, or a government id. Google’s privacy policy describes what Google does with a purchase.

This version launches with every letter and number open. The child is not shown a purchase.

What we do not collect

Hurman does not collect or share:

  • name, email, or phone number of a child or a parent
  • precise or approximate location
  • contacts, photos, microphone audio, or camera
  • an advertising id, or any id used to track a person across apps
  • diagnostics, crash reports, or analytics

The app does not show ads. It does not include an advertising SDK, an analytics SDK, or a crash-reporting SDK.

The child cannot type, chat, or share a drawing.

Permissions

The app can use the internet to fetch the public settings file, and so Google Play can handle an optional purchase. That connection does not upload a child’s progress. The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions, and it does not use the advertising-id permission.

Reset and deletion

Erase, in the grown-up screen, deletes stars and stickers on that phone.

Uninstalling Harf Garden deletes the on-device database. There is nothing of yours on a Harf Garden server to delete, because the app does not keep an account or a copy of progress. A purchase made through Google Play, if there ever is one, stays on the Google account until you manage it in Google Play.

Children

Harf Garden is made for children under 13, and specifically for ages 3–6. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not collect personal information from anyone else either.

Changes

The date at the top changes when this policy changes.
